# Economics calculation basis

KDP Scout's economics panel is an **estimate** for the Amazon.com marketplace and should be verified in Amazon KDP before production.

## Validation thresholds

The project's controlling `Amazon KDP Validation Method` defines:

- A practical initial success threshold of at least **$500/month in gross royalties for six consecutive post-launch months**.
- A strong title as at least **$1,000/month in gross royalties**.
- Economics inputs should include target paperback/Kindle prices, printing cost, royalties, advertising assumptions, production cost, and ongoing update/review cost.

## Amazon.com paperback basis

Current KDP paperback royalty formula:

`(royalty rate × list price) - printing cost = royalty`

Current Amazon.com royalty tiers used by the panel:

- 50% for list prices at or below $9.98
- 60% for list prices at or above $9.99

Current Amazon.com print-cost formulas used by the panel:

- Black ink, regular trim, 24–110 pages: $2.30 fixed
- Black ink, large trim, 24–110 pages: $2.84 fixed
- Black ink, 111–828 pages: $1.00 + pages × $0.012 regular / $0.017 large
- Standard color, 72–600 pages: $1.00 + pages × $0.0255 regular / $0.0402 large
- Premium color, 24–40 pages: $3.60 regular / $4.20 large
- Premium color, 42–828 pages: $1.00 + pages × $0.065 regular / $0.080 large

KDP defines large trim for this cost table as more than 6.12 inches wide or more than 9 inches high.

## Kindle basis

The panel supports both 35% and 70% royalty options. The simplified 70% estimate assumes an eligible sale with no VAT and subtracts the user-editable delivery-cost estimate. The current Amazon.com 70% list-price band is $2.99–$12.99; other KDP eligibility requirements still apply.

## Important limitation

KDP rates and policies can change. The panel therefore links to the official KDP Printing Cost & Royalty Calculator and should not be treated as the final publishing quote.
